# Orderly Convergence

**Scenario ID:** `orderly_convergence`
**Version:** 2026-Q2-v1
**Published:** 16 May 2026
**Role in scenario set:** Upside boundary, lower-probability than Muddle Through but a meaningful upside anchor.

---

## The scenario in one paragraph

The set of conditions for benign resolution all come together. Cost-of-living pressure ebbs as inflation cools to target — not because central banks crush demand but because supply-side normalisation and AI-enabled productivity gains absorb the wage growth that drove sticky services inflation. Real rates settle modestly positive, comfortably above pre-2022 but defensible. The political reset that climate policy needed actually happens: regional governments rebuild coalitions around coordinated transition, periodic backsliding gives way to renewed momentum, the disorderly-by-default path is averted. Geopolitical tensions resolve materially — meaningful US-China stabilisation, Middle East de-escalation, sanctions architecture partly unwinds. The US continues to lead growth but the lift propagates this time, pulling Europe out of its stagnation and supporting EM recovery. AI is the productivity story that works — enterprise adoption accelerates beyond the early-adopter cohort, rents diffuse to broader users, the productivity surprise supports lower inflation and higher real growth simultaneously. Equity-market growth-stock multiples re-expand. Demographic adaptation proceeds in an orderly way too, with African skills-development and corridor establishment beginning to address the structural challenge. The world that the IMF reference forecast and the OECD baseline implicitly assume — that almost no one privately bets on.

## Macro picture

Global growth recovers to 3.0–3.2% — robust by post-pandemic standards. US growth at 2.5%, Europe pulling out of stagnation to 1.8–2.2%, EM at 4–5%. Headline inflation in advanced economies returns to 2.3% by year 3, near central-bank targets. Real policy rates settle at 0.8% — modestly positive, above the 2010s zero-bound but well below Muddle Through's 1.0%+. The USD softens modestly as the Fed eases; EM currencies recover; capital flows reglobalise.

## US powerhouse stance

The US continues to lead growth but in this scenario the lift propagates. US productivity gains (from AI working economy-wide) translate into broader trade volumes that support Europe and EM. The US powerhouse becomes the engine of *global* recovery rather than a bifurcating outperformer.

## Climate policy posture

Political reset restores coordinated transition. Carbon-pricing trajectories reaffirmed and extended; renewables / EV / transition-minerals subsidies stabilised on long-term frameworks; cross-border-adjustment mechanisms harmonised across major economies. The disorderly path the world is currently on is reversed via political will rather than crisis. The aggregate emissions trajectory bends downward toward IEA STEPS or modestly better.

## AI and productivity

The bull case for AI delivers. Aggregate TFP grows 1.5–2.0% — the productivity surprise materialises. Enterprise AI adoption broadens beyond the early-adopter cohort; integration challenges get solved; trust and audit frameworks evolve to support deployment in regulated industries; rents diffuse from platform owners to broader user base over the medium term. Compounding gains. Growth-stock multiples re-expand. Labour-cost containment continues but is no longer the main story — productivity transmission is.

## Geopolitics and trade

Meaningful US-China stabilisation: trade frameworks negotiated, military tensions managed, technology export controls relaxed selectively. Middle East de-escalation with a security architecture refresh. Russia-NATO standoff de-escalates over the medium term. Bilateral trade-barrier reductions; tariffs partly rolled back; export-control regimes simplified to focus on genuinely dual-use technology. Supply chain regionalisation slows; partial reglobalisation in lower-sensitivity categories.

## Demographic response

Orderly here too. African skills-development gains traction with international support; migration corridors begin to establish; robotics deployment proceeds in advanced economies. Doesn't solve the long-term demographic question (the 2040s skilled-migration cliff is structural and beyond this scenario's horizon), but the trajectory toward solving it is being walked. China continues to lead on robotics; advanced economies absorb the demographic challenge via a combination of AI / immigration / Africa-corridor establishment.

## Sectoral picture

Broad-based equity-market gains with growth-stock leadership. Cyclical industrials lift on global recovery; energy investment cycle stable; financials benefit from credit-cycle benignity; growth equities re-rate up as productivity story validates; defensives lag as risk appetite returns. Transition sectors (renewables, EVs, transition minerals) attract sustained capital as policy frameworks stabilise.

## How the world becomes Orderly Convergence

The conditions stack: cost-of-living pressure abates enough for climate-policy political reset; central banks succeed in re-anchoring inflation expectations; geopolitical actors negotiate meaningful trade frameworks; AI enterprise adoption broadens through resolved integration / trust / audit challenges; African skills-development and corridor establishment begin. Each is individually plausible; their compounding probability is what makes this the upside boundary rather than the central case.

## What would invalidate this scenario

Continued sticky inflation despite supply normalisation (would shift toward Muddle Through). Inflation-expectations un-anchoring (would shift toward Stagflation Persists). Geopolitical escalation (would shift toward Fragmentation). Major physical-loss event or carbon-policy crisis (would shift toward Disorderly Climate Crystallisation). Persistent enterprise-AI adoption lag despite continued investment (would shift toward AI Productivity Lag).

## Implications for DNL, CSL, WBC (brief)

**DNL** — mining capex cycle healthy; commodity demand robust; energy-input costs normalised; long-term gas contracts still helpful but less critical; AI helps mining-customer productivity; modest demand growth on top of cycle. Upside vs Muddle Through is moderate.

**CSL** — strongest upside of the three. Healthcare spend healthy; pipeline-NPV multiples expand as growth-stock premium returns; AI accelerates drug-discovery economics; labour-cost containment continues; multiple-expansion compounds with productivity-driven earnings growth. The scenario where CSL re-rates upward materially.

**WBC** — credit losses at cycle lows; loan growth normalises to GDP+; deposit franchise intact; AI improves cost-to-income; capital returns flow through. Positive but limited — banks don't get the same multiple expansion as growth stocks; the scenario is supportive but not transformative.
