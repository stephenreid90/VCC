When each mine runs (D-98)

Each mine produces at its FY27 guidance rate until its stated life ends. The same in every scenario. Bert and the Northparkes recovery projects are added output from FY29. Mt Rawdon, Marsden and Carnaby are left out.

Mine inputs

AISC is the company's all-in sustaining cost per ounce of gold, after copper credits. A negative AISC means copper more than pays for the mine.
