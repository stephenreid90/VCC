# Banking Valuation Adjustments

## Why Banks are Different

- Banks use leverage as raw material → EV/EBITDA doesn't apply
- Preferred method: **P/E**, **P/Book**, or **Residual Income (RI)**
- Key drivers: NIM, loan growth, credit quality, CET1

## Adjusted Metrics

### NIM Adjustment (`net_interest_margin_adjustment`)
Adjust NIM for interest rate cycle. Rising rates → NIM expansion (+5%); falling → compression (-5%).

### Loan Loss Reserves (`loan_loss_reserve_adjustment`)
Expected loss = Total Loans × Default Rate × (1 - Recovery Rate)

### CET1 Adequacy (`cet1_ratio_check`)
Check headroom above APRA/Basel minimums. Headroom drives dividend capacity.

### Dividend Capacity (`dividend_capacity`)
Estimate distributable earnings after maintaining CET1 target.
